# Source census

Counts from the Sanskrit Wikisource crawl of श्रीमद्भागवतपुराणम्, after transform into `content/mula/`. The viewer explorer reports the verse total.

Verse rows are numbered units (`॥ n ॥`, `। n ॥`, a number at the end of the line, or `॥ 10.30.001 ॥`). Chapter colophons (`इति श्री…`) are kept as `colophon` blocks and are not in the verse total.

Skandha 12, adhyāya 13 of this text calls the Bhāgavata अष्टादशसाहस्रम् (18,000). The numbered units on these pages add up to 14,216.
