## Sandhi speakers still inside the verse

Spaced `उवाच` / `ऊचुः` lines are `speaker` blocks, and `annotations/` carries `speaker` and `meter` spans for the explorer. About 235 short sandhi forms are still inside the verse, for example `कलिरुवाच` at skandha 1, adhyāya 17, verse 36. Lift those lines before extending the spans.

Narrative speech that is part of the śloka stays inside the verse.
